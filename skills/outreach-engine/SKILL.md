## CHANNEL RULES

### Channel 1: EMAIL (Primary, highest conversion)
**When to use:** Decision-makers with verified emails
**Frequency:** 3-5 touch points over 14 days
**Response rate:** 2-5% (cold), 5-15% (warm)
**Tone:** Professional, benefit-focused, clear CTA

### Channel 2: LINKEDIN (Secondary, social proof)
**When to use:** After email 1, if no response
**Frequency:** 2-3 message, 1-2 connection requests (spaced)
**Response rate:** 1-3% (cold), 5-10% (warm)
**Tone:** Professional, peer-to-peer, industry insights

### Channel 3: WHATSAPP (Tertiary, highest engagement if you have number)
**When to use:** After email 1-2, only with confirmed mobile
**Frequency:** 1-2 casual message (not salesy)
**Response rate:** 10-20% (warm up only)
**Tone:** Casual, value-first, no hard sell

## EMAIL SEQUENCE TEMPLATE

**3-Email Sequence (14 days):**

```
Day 1, 9am (Intro):
Subject: [Personalized hook about their company/challenge]
Body:
  Hi [First Name],
  
  I noticed [specific insight about their company] — was curious 
  if [your value prop] is something you've explored?
  
  [One sentence on why you're reaching out to them specifically]
  
  Open to a quick call?
  
  [Your name]
```

**Day 4, 10am (Value angle):**
```
Subject: Re: [Previous subject — same thread]
Body:
  Hi [First Name],
  
  Quick follow-up on my last note.
  
  Most [their role/industry] we talk to are [common challenge].
  We helped [similar company] [specific result].
  
  Curious if that's relevant?
  
  [CTA link]
  
  [Your name]
```

**Day 12, 2pm (Social proof + urgency):**
```
Subject: One last check-in: [benefit]
Body:
  Hi [First Name],
  
  Last attempt here — didn't want to keep pinging.
  
  We just helped [company in their industry] [result].
  Thought you should know it exists.
  
  If not a fit now, no worries. Happy to reconnect later.
  
  [CTA or calendly link]
  
  [Your name]
```

## PERSONALIZATION RULES

**High-personalization (hot leads, score 0.85+):**
- Mention their recent hire (from signals)
- Reference their blog post / LinkedIn post
- Compliment their product feature
- Name specific pain point they likely have

**Medium-personalization (warm leads, score 0.65-0.85):**
- Mention their company name + size
- Reference their industry
- Generic but targeted pain point

**Low-personalization (cold leads, score <0.65):**
- Use template as-is
- A/B test different openers
- Measure response rate

**Personalization checklist:**
- [ ] First name (John, not "hey there")
- [ ] Company name
- [ ] Their recent activity (hired, funded, product launch)
- [ ] Specific pain point they'd have
- [ ] Why you're reaching out to *them* specifically

## SUBJECT LINE FORMULAS

**High engagement (use these):**
- "[Their company] + [their challenge] + [your benefit]"
  Example: "HubSpot's sales hiring challenge — we solved it for 3 similar startups"
  
- "[Specific insight] about [their space]"
  Example: "What's driving sales team expansion at companies like [their company]"
  
- "Quick question about [their recent signal]"
  Example: "Quick thought on your Series A funding round"

**Lower engagement (avoid):**
- Generic: "Quick question", "Connection", "Hey"
- Clickbait: "This one weird trick", "Companies hate this"
- Urgency-only: "Urgent", "Limited time", "Act now"

## CTA (Call-to-action) OPTIONS

**High friction (book call directly):**
```
[Click here to grab a 15 min on my calendar]
[your-calendly-link]
```
⚠️ Use only for warm + hot leads (score 0.75+)

**Medium friction (email first):**
```
Open to a quick call? Just reply with "yes" and I'll send calendar options.
```
✓ Use for most leads

**Low friction (educational link):**
```
I wrote a 2-min guide on this: [link]
Let me know what you think.
```
✓ Use for cold leads to warm them up first

## RESPONSE HANDLING

**If they respond:**
```
Email response → Move to sales-pipeline skill (booking, deal tracking)
LinkedIn response → Send calendar link, transition to email
WhatsApp response → Assess fit, then move to sales-pipeline
```

**If they unsubscribe:**
```
Remove from outreach immediately
Add to "Not interested" list (don't re-engage for 6 months)
Log reason if provided
```

**If they say "not interested":**
```
Say: "Totally understand. If things change, I'll be here."
Add to "Nurture" list (touch every 3 months with relevant content)
```

## A/B TESTING

**Test email subject lines:**
- Group A: Personalized (mention company + challenge)
- Group B: Benefit-focused (emphasize outcome)
- Split: 50/50 of first 20 leads
- Measure: Open rate (target > 35%), Response rate (target > 2%)
- Winner: Use that subject line template for remaining leads

**Test CTAs:**
- Group A: "Let's chat" (vague)
- Group B: "15 min call?" (specific)
- Measure: Click rate, calendar bookings
- Winner: Use winning CTA template

**Test frequencies:**
- Campaign A: 3 emails over 10 days
- Campaign B: 3 emails over 14 days (slower)
- Measure: Response rate + unsubscribe rate
- Winner: Slower = lower unsubscribe if conversion similar

## ANTI-PATTERNS

❌ **Don't send all 3 emails in 1 day.**
Looks spammy. 3-4 day gaps between emails.

❌ **Don't use generic templates for hot leads.**
You found their signals, use them. Personalize.

❌ **Don't mix WhatsApp with sales pitch.**
WhatsApp is for warm engagement, not "buy now". 

❌ **Don't ignore unsubscribes.**
Mark immediately and don't re-engage.

❌ **Don't use WeirdCapitalization or TOO MANY EXCLAMATION MARKS!!!**
Looks unprofessional. Use normal business email tone.
