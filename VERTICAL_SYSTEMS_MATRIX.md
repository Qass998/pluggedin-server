## FRAMEWORK

For each vertical:
1. **Decision maker** (who we actually reach)
2. **Business model** (how they make money, what they care about)
3. **Lead sources** (where to find prospects)
4. **Outreach channel** (email, phone, LinkedIn, Google Ads)
5. **Systems/Skills** (which PluggedIN services apply)
6. **Positioning** (how we introduce ourselves)
7. **Success metric** (what "outcome" means to them)

## B2B VERTICALS (Decision: Corporate buyer, budget approval)

### 1. B2B SAAS (Sales teams, GTM leaders)

**Decision maker:** VP Sales, Head of Sales, Revenue Ops
**What they care about:** Pipeline velocity, deal close rate, cost per booking
**How they make money:** Recurring revenue, ACV growth, expansion

**Lead sources:**
- LinkedIn (target companies hiring sales roles)
- Funding databases (recent Series A/B raises)
- Job boards (expanding sales team = hiring)
- Vibe Prospecting MCP (firmographics + signals)

**Outreach channel:** LinkedIn (DM to VP Sales) + Email
**Timing:** Monday-Wednesday 9-11am

**Systems/Skills:**
- Pipeline Agent (£997) — lead discovery, qualification, booking
- Sales Intelligence (£697) — call coaching, forecasting
- Intelligence Agent (£697) — signal monitoring (hiring, funding)
- Data Intelligence (£897) — KPI tracking, board reports

**Positioning strategy:**
```
"Hi [Name], I'm [Agent Name] with PluggedIN. 
I work with B2B SaaS teams on pipeline acceleration. 

I noticed [Company] just raised [funding amount] and 
is posting for [number] new sales roles — usually means 
you're scaling GTM fast.

I help teams like yours do 3 things:
1. Generate qualified pipeline (200-300/month)
2. Improve close rates (30-40% better through coaching)
3. Forecast accurately (90%+ forecast confidence)

Would 15 minutes be worth it to see how we're doing this 
for similar companies?"
```

**How to introduce:** **"I'm [Agent Name], PluggedIN's growth specialist"** 
(Position as PluggedIN expert reaching out, not pretending to be their employee)

**Why this works:** VPs appreciate transparency. They know outsourced teams exist. They care about results.

## INTRODUCTION STRATEGY: THE DECISION

### Option A: Transparent About PluggedIN
```
"Hi [Name], I'm [Agent Name] with PluggedIN. 
I specialize in helping [vertical] companies with [outcome].
We work with [Company] and I think we could help you too."
```
**Pros:** Honest, builds PluggedIN brand, ethical
**Cons:** SMBs don't know what PluggedIN is, B2B expects outsourced teams

**Use for:** B2B SaaS, Legal, Enterprise-focused

---

### Option B: Position as Their Specialist (No company name)
```
"Hi [Name], I'm [Agent Name]. I specialize in helping 
[vertical] companies [outcome]. 

I work with [Competitor] and [Competitor] on this.
Would a quick conversation make sense?"
```
**Pros:** Focuses on expertise, builds credibility in vertical, feels personal
**Cons:** Less brand building for PluggedIN

**Use for:** SMB (roofing, ecommerce, restaurants), mid-market trades

---

### Option C: Hybrid (PluggedIN specialist for their vertical)
```
"Hi [Name], I'm [Agent Name] from PluggedIN. 
I specialize exclusively in [vertical] growth.

My job is to help companies like you [specific outcome].
I work with [Company] on exactly this.
Would 15 minutes be worth exploring?"
```
**Pros:** Honest + specialized + outcome-focused
**Cons:** Takes more words

**Use for:** High-trust B2B (legal, professional services), mature verticals

---

### Option D: Ultra-Direct / Conversational (SMB focus)
```
"Hey [Name] — quick question. Are you looking to get 
more [outcome] for [business]?

I help companies like yours do exactly that. 
Worth a 15-min call?"
```
**Pros:** Disarming, casual, SMB-friendly, low friction
**Cons:** Informal, might feel too casual for corporate

**Use for:** SMB trades, ecommerce, local services
