## LEGAL SERVICES (Law Firms, Solicitors, Legal Tech)

### Recommended Service Bundle
- **Pipeline Agent** (£997) — find clients needing legal services
- **Presence Agent** (£797) — never miss a client inquiry
- **Intelligence Agent** (£697) — track competitor moves, regulatory changes

**Total:** £2,491/month → offer at Growth Package (£2,497)

### ICP Profile
```
Target: Law firms seeking to expand practice areas
Ideal client:
  - Law firm size: 10-100 lawyers
  - Practice areas: Corporate, IP, Litigation, Employment
  - Geography: UK-based (or expand)
  - Growth trigger: Hiring new partners, opening new offices
  - Budget: £2k-5k/month for business development
```

### Pipeline Agent Configuration
```
Lead Sources:
  ✓ LinkedIn (search: "partner at law firm", "corporate counsel")
  ✓ Legal directories (LawNet, Law Society, Chambers)
  ✓ Company registries (find GCs in expansion mode)
  ✓ Event attendees (legal conferences, networking)

Lead Discovery Frequency: Daily (5am)
Target: 50-100 leads/week

Enrichment:
  - Registry lookups (Companies House) — free
  - Email finding (Hunter.io)
  - LinkedIn profile deep dive

Outreach Sequence:
  Day 1: Email intro (subject: "Partnership opportunity for [practice area]")
  Day 3: LinkedIn message (personalized to their firm + practice area)
  Day 7: Email follow-up 1 (case study: similar law firm success)
  Day 14: Email follow-up 2 (limited-time offer)

Qualification Rules:
  - Partner/Senior Associate role? (weight: 40%)
  - Hiring or expansion signals? (weight: 30%)
  - Practice area match? (weight: 20%)
  - Firm size 10-100? (weight: 10%)
```

### Presence Agent Configuration
```
Greeting: "Hi, this is [Firm Name]. How can we assist you?"
Behavior:
  - Take detailed notes (case type, urgency, deadline)
  - Identify decision-maker (partner, practice manager)
  - Book consultation (30 min slots)
  
Available: 9am-6pm GMT, Monday-Friday
Auto-book: Partners and in-house counsel (hot prospects)

WhatsApp:
  - Send: initial inquiry acknowledgment
  - Alert director: urgent deadline cases
```

### Intelligence Agent Configuration
```
Signals to Monitor:
  ✓ Hiring (law firm posting roles = growth)
  ✓ Regulatory changes (affects demand)
  ✓ Competitor announcements (new offices, mergers)
  ✓ Practice area trends (emerging legal needs)

Competitors to Watch: Top 5 firms in target market

Briefing: Weekly video (Monday 6am)
  - Top 3 hiring signals (competitors expanding)
  - Regulatory updates (what affects your clients)
  - New market opportunities

CRM Table: Law Firm Prospects
  Fields: Firm name, location, practice areas, headcount, last job posting
```

### Expected Results
- **200 leads/month** from legal directories + LinkedIn
- **15 consultations booked/month**
- **Cost per consultation:** £150-200
- **Conversion to client:** ~20-30% → 3-5 new client relationships/month

### CRM Integration
- **CRM Type:** HubSpot (most law firms use this)
- **Tables:**
  - Contacts (prospects: partners, in-house counsel)
  - Deals (potential engagements)
  - Activities (calls, meetings, proposals)
- **Sample data:** 500+ law firms pre-seeded for lookup

## TEMPLATE SWITCHING

**To use a template:**

3. Pre-fill service bundle + ICP + lead sources

Each template is a starting point. In onboarding call:
